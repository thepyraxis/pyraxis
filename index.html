      07 · Feedback + Reviews

      One question after every visit. Two endings.

      Both endings play below — and notice: the public review link is offered to every customer, in both.

      Animated demonstration of the review system. After each visit, every customer is asked for a star rating and offered the same easy path to share publicly. A five-star rating flows toward the public Google review, which types itself out and becomes social proof. A two-star rating raises a private alert to the owner — while the public review link remains offered — the issue is fixed and the customer is won back.

      21:14

      WHAT THE SYSTEM DID

        ANY RATING → honest feedback captured → the owner listens

        HAPPY CUSTOMER → easy public sharing → new customers

        A PROBLEM → private alert → fixed fast → won back

        Every customer is asked — and every customer gets the same easy path to share publicly, good or bad. Problems also travel privately to the owner so they get fixed. PYRAXIS never writes, filters or suppresses reviews: every experience becomes useful information.
