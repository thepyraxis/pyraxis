      Reviews

      Every review has a destination.

      Positive feedback is shared. Critical feedback is routed for resolution. Both are handled systematically.

      Animated demonstration of the review system. After each visit, every customer is asked for a star rating and offered the same easy path to share publicly. A five-star rating flows toward the public Google review, which types itself out and becomes social proof. A two-star rating raises a private alert to the owner — while the public review link remains offered — the issue is fixed and the customer is won back.
