Privacy Policy

1. Overview

At PYRAXIS, we value your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle information when you interact with our growth systems and website.

2. Information Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us through our website contact form, WhatsApp, email, or discovery call bookings. This includes your name, contact details, and business information required to design your growth strategy.

3. How enquiries reach us

When you use the request form, the details you enter (name, business type, what you want help with, and your WhatsApp number and/or email) are only used to build a message on your own device. Pressing “Request my system” opens WhatsApp with that message pre-filled. The website does not send the form to any server or form-delivery service, does not store your entries, and nothing reaches us until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages you send there. The “WhatsApp us” buttons work the same way, with a short pre-filled greeting.

4. Usage of Data

Your data is used solely to provide services, improve system performance, and communicate regarding your project. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We do not run analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies on this site. All scripts, fonts, images, and 3D artwork are served from our own pages, so the site itself makes no requests to third parties. If you choose to continue on WhatsApp, you leave this site and WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply.

6. Data retention & your requests

We keep enquiry conversations only as long as needed to respond and, where a project follows, to deliver it. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it, at thepyraxis@gmail.com — we will act within 30 days.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at thepyraxis@gmail.com.
