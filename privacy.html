Privacy Policy

1. Overview

At PYRAXIS, we value your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle information when you interact with our growth systems and website.

2. Information Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us through WhatsApp, Email, or Discovery Call bookings. This includes your name, contact details, and business information required to design your growth strategy.

3. How enquiries reach us

This website has no backend and no accounts. When you submit the contact form, your browser opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message containing exactly what you entered. Nothing is sent to us until you review that message and press send inside WhatsApp. From that point, your enquiry is handled as a WhatsApp conversation, subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to this one.

4. Usage of Data

Your data is used solely to provide services, improve system performance, and communicate regarding your project. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We do not run analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies on this site, and all scripts, images, and 3D artwork are served from our own pages. The only external requests the site makes are for fonts (Google Fonts), which may log standard technical data such as IP addresses under Google's own policy.

6. Data retention & your requests

We keep enquiry conversations only as long as needed to respond and, where a project follows, to deliver it. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it, at thepyraxis@gmail.com — we will act within 30 days.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related inquiries, contact us at thepyraxis@gmail.com.
